Microeconomics Course Topics & Visual Models

Comprehensive visual infographics, market structures, elasticity models, and consumer behavior diagrams for Microeconomics course.

Module 01

Topic 1: Introduction to Economics & PPF

Scarcity, opportunity costs, factors of production, and the Production Possibility Frontier (PPF) model.

Module 02

Topic 2: Demand, Supply & Market Equilibrium

Law of demand and supply, market equilibrium mechanism, surpluses, shortages, and price controls.

Module 03

Topic 3: Elasticity & Its Applications

Price elasticity of demand (PED), income elasticity, cross-price elasticity, and revenue impact.

Module 04

Topic 4: Theory of Consumer Choice

Utility maximization, indifference curves, budget constraints, and marginal rate of substitution (MRS).

Module 05

Topic 5: Production Costs & Firm Behavior

Short-run vs long-run production costs, fixed/variable costs, marginal cost (MC), and economies of scale.

Module 06

Topic 6: Perfectly Competitive Markets

Profit maximization (MR=MC), price takers, short-run shutdown decisions, and long-run equilibrium.

Module 07

Topic 7: Pure Monopoly & Market Power

Monopoly pricing, barriers to entry, deadweight loss, price discrimination, and public policy regulation.

Module 08

Topic 8: Monopolistic Competition

Product differentiation, non-price competition, advertising dynamics, and long-run zero economic profit.

Module 09

Topic 9: Oligopoly & Strategic Interaction

Game theory, Prisoner's Dilemma, Nash equilibrium, cartels, and interdependence among market leaders.

Module 10

Topic 10: Externalities & Market Failure

Positive and negative externalities, Pigouvian taxes, Coase theorem, public goods, and free-rider problems.

Module 11

Topic 11: Factor Markets & Labor Demand

Marginal revenue product of labor (MRPL), wage determination, labor supply curve, and monopsony power.

Module 12

Topic 12: Asymmetric Information & Risk

Adverse selection, moral hazard, principal-agent problems, signaling mechanisms, and insurance market dynamics.

Module 13

Topic 13: Intro to Behavioral Economics

Bounded rationality, cognitive biases, loss aversion, anchoring, heuristic decision-making, and nudge theory.

Module 14

Topic 14: General Equilibrium & Welfare

Pareto efficiency, Edgeworth box diagram, welfare economic theorems, and overall resource allocation efficiency.
