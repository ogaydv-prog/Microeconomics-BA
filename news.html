Fonterra's Strategic Pivot: Divestment & Profit Doubling

Market Dynamics: New Zealand giant Fonterra reported a 115%+ surge in annual net profit following the strategic sale of consumer brand assets (including Mainland).

Microeconomic Rationale: By divesting low-margin consumer retail operations, Fonterra reallocated capital toward high-margin B2B ingredients and specialized dairy nutrition, reducing fixed distribution overhead and optimizing EBIT margins.
